Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 15, 2026

At AI BI Platform, we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal data and uploaded datasets. This Privacy Policy details how we handle, store, and secure your information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information required to create accounts and process datasets:

  • Account Data: Email address, usernames, passwords (stored using secure bcrypt hashing), and display names.
  • Dataset Uploads: CSV, Excel, and XLS files processed using python panda utilities.
  • Application Telemetry: Log operations, diagnostic errors, and pipeline performance metrics.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is processed strictly to perform the calculations requested by you:

  • To profile column metadata, calculate descriptive stats, and build correlation matrices.
  • To run AutoML training models and generate predictions.
  • To manage login sessions securely using stateless JWT refresh and access tokens.

3. Data Retention and Deletion

Uploaded datasets are stored locally in the application storage directory. When you choose to delete a dataset or engineered feature set via the dashboard, the corresponding record and physical file are permanently deleted from our servers immediately.

4. Cookies

We use strict session cookies to transmit JWT refresh tokens securely (`refresh_token`). These cookies are configured as HTTPOnly and Lax to protect your sessions against Cross-Site Scripting (XSS) and Cross-Site Request Forgery (CSRF).

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, share, or trade your datasets or personal account information with any third-party marketing services or external platforms.
